ANSWERS TO COMMON QUESTIONS

Good questions. Clear answers.

Do you support all printer brands?

We assist with issues involving a wide range of consumer and business printer brands, including HP, Canon, Epson, Brother, Xerox, Lexmark, Ricoh, and more. Available solutions can vary by model.

Can I get printer help anywhere in the USA?

Remote troubleshooting assistance is offered nationwide. On-site service depends on technician availability in your location.

Can you help if my printer says it's offline?

Yes. We can help investigate network connectivity, device settings, printer status, queues, and other common causes of an offline printer.

How can I speak to someone about my printer?
